Modeling Procrastination · Behavioral Research System
DEPLOYED

The existing tools couldn’t see the process — so I built one that could.

Built: a standardized 14-node browser-based simulation that records decision-by-decision behavior under identical academic stressors and turns those choices into behavioral trajectories.

What I found: high- and low-delay trajectories diverged early, before final outcomes appeared. No single decision explained the difference; small moments of hesitation accumulated across the sequence.

RESEARCH → PRODUCT → IP The simulation’s process-level view of procrastination became the foundation for FocusPal.

What began as a study of accumulating micro-hesitations became a shipped behavioral intervention product — and later a U.S. Non-Provisional Patent Application.
